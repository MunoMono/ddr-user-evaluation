
# Before you begin

This page explains what you need before using the DDR Digital Archive.

## What you will need

To use the application, you will need:

- A desktop or laptop computer with an internet connection.
- A modern web browser, such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox or Safari.
- The web address of the DDR Digital Archive.
- Login details, if access to the current application requires authentication.

No specialist software, programming knowledge or previous experience of archival databases is required.

## Accessing the application

The DDR Digital Archive is available at:

[Open the DDR Digital Archive](https://ddrarchive.org)

Some areas or preview environments may require a user account.

If you have been provided with login details, use those credentials when prompted.

Do not share your password with other people or include it in screenshots, emails or feedback documents.

## Understanding the archive

You do not need previous knowledge of the Department of Design Research to begin exploring.

However, it may help to understand that the archive contains information about historical people, organisations, research projects and documents.

Some information may be incomplete or uncertain. This reflects the nature of the surviving historical evidence and does not necessarily indicate a problem with the application.
