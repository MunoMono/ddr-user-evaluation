
# Troubleshooting

This page provides guidance if you encounter difficulties accessing or navigating the DDR Digital Archive.

## The website does not open

Check that your computer is connected to the internet.

Make sure you are using the correct web address:

[https://ddrarchive.org](https://ddrarchive.org)

If the page does not load, try refreshing your browser.

If the problem continues, try opening the application in another browser.

## I cannot sign in

Check that you have entered the correct email address and password.

Passwords are case-sensitive, so check whether Caps Lock is enabled.

If you have forgotten your password, select **Reset password** on the login screen and follow the instructions.

If you have not been given an account or cannot complete the sign-in process, contact the person who provided access to the application.

## The page looks different from the screenshot

The application is being developed as part of an ongoing research project.

Some controls, labels or layouts may change between versions.

Look for the control described in the written instructions. If you cannot find it, avoid guessing and seek clarification.

## A page appears empty or does not respond

Try refreshing the page.

If the problem persists, make a note of what you were attempting to do and which page you were using.

Where appropriate, capture a screenshot showing the problem.

Do not include passwords, personal information or sensitive material in screenshots.

## I cannot find the information I expected

The DDR Digital Archive is an evolving research resource.

Not all historical material has been digitised, described or incorporated into the application.

An unsuccessful search does not necessarily mean that the information does not exist.

Try a different name or search term where the interface allows it.

## Reporting a problem

If you encounter a problem that prevents you from continuing, record:

- The page or section you were using.
- What you were trying to do.
- What happened instead.
- Any error message displayed.
- A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem.

Share this information through the contact or support arrangements provided for your access.

Do not include passwords or confidential information.
