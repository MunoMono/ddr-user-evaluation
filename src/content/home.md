
# DDR user evaluation

## Welcome

This website provides guidance and supporting information for exploring three digital research applications developed as part of a practice-led PhD at the Royal College of Art.

The research examines the history of the RCA's Department of Design Research (1965–1985), investigating how surviving archival materials can be made more accessible, connected and useful for contemporary inquiry.

The applications bring together archival description, digital access and computational approaches to investigating historical evidence.

## About this documentation

The website contains three types of information:

- **User guide**–Instructions for navigating and using the applications.
- **Evaluation tasks**–Structured activities used to explore how participants understand and engage with the applications.
- **Evaluation information**–Background information about the evaluation and participation arrangements.

The documentation is organised by application. Select one of the three sections above to begin.

## Research context

The work is being undertaken by Graham Newman as part of his doctoral research at the Royal College of Art.

The digital environment is an evolving research outcome. Its content, functionality and documentation may change as the research develops.
